** +
** =initiateSub= installs a slim subproject overlay --- only =CLAUDE.md= (symlink to =mother/initiateSub/CLAUDE.md=), =AI-Activity.org=, =AI-DevStatus.org=, =AI-WorkPlan.org=.
** Does NOT install =AI-WORKFLOW.org=, or =.claude/= --- those are inherited from an ancestor via Claude Code's =CLAUDE.md= walk-up.
** Preconditions: an ancestor directory must have been =initiate=-d already, and the target directory must not already have a =CLAUDE.md=.
** =--activity= is required (no inheritance-from-parent default).
** Nested =initiateSub= is supported --- a subproject's =CLAUDE.md= satisfies the walk-up precondition for a sub-subproject.
** =aiSuspend= / =aiResume= are mode-aware: =aiSuspend= stashes =CLAUDE.md= as =CLAUDE.md.dormant= (preserving symlink target as mode signature); =aiResume= reads it to choose base vs sub restore.
** -B|

** +
** When an activity ships a =_skills/= directory, =initiate= symlinks it to =.claude/skills/= alongside =settings.json= and =commands=.
** Skills are folders each containing a =SKILL.md= with YAML front-matter (=name:=, =description:=) followed by markdown content. Claude Code discovers them and loads their content only when the current task matches the description.
** The =bisos-pip= activity currently ships five skills:
**  - =writing-cs-commands= --- authoring =cs.Cmnd= subclasses (params, args, classHead dblock, common patterns).
**  - =dblock-authoring= --- poly-dblock =####+BEGIN:= blocks, =csuListProc= pattern, dblock-based inline unit tests.
**  - =readme-authorship= --- =README.org= authorship and org→rst conversion for PyPI.
**  - =blee-panel-authorship= --- standard =fullUsagePanel-en.org= skeleton and dblock content policy.
**  - =bisos-pip-release= --- end-to-end PyPI release checklist.
** Authoring a new skill: add a folder under =<templates-base>/<activity>/_skills/<skill-name>/= with a =SKILL.md= inside. If applicable to every activity, place under =mother/_skills/= instead.
** -B|
